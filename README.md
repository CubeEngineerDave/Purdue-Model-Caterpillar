This paper investigates the Purdue Model for Industrial Control System (ICS) cybersecurity and examines its application within large manufacturing organizations such as Caterpillar, Komatsu, and John Deere. The Purdue Model provides a layered architecture that separates Information Technology (IT) and Operational Technology (OT) environments, reducing cyber risk by controlling communication pathways between enterprise systems and industrial control systems. This paper analyzes the model’s structure, explains the importance of segmentation, evaluates system performance through modern Security Operations Center (SOC) tools, and identifies key vendors supplying hardware and software used across Purdue levels. The goal is to demonstrate how the Purdue Model can be adapted to strengthen industrial cybersecurity defenses against modern threats.
